## Industry Body / Trade Resource

- **SA Sports Trader** — B2B magazine for the sport/outdoor/leisure trade (since 1978). Their annual **Activewear, Sport and Outdoor Retail Directory** lists ~500 suppliers across 860 categories — the most comprehensive trade-side directory in the country. ([sportstrader.co.za](https://www.sportstrader.co.za/))
- **SAFLEC** — South African Footwear and Leather Export Council. ([saflec.co.za](https://saflec.co.za/))

For institutional bulk listings, the Sports Trader retail directory is the single best paid resource to acquire (annual publication, mailed with Q4 issue).

## Notes

- This list is grouped by primary specialty; many retailers (e.g. Sportsmans Warehouse, Decathlon, Makro, Takealot) cover multiple categories.
- Regional independents (single-store/town shops) are not exhaustively listed — Sports Trader's directory captures the long tail.
- For the subset where named decision-makers and direct contacts were sourced, see `inertia-wave-sa-resellers.md`.
