# Inertia Wave South Africa — Potential Reseller Pipeline

**Client:** Inertia Wave South Africa (Breathe108 t/a Inertia Wave SA — `wave@inertiawave.co.za`, inertiawave.co.za)
**Product:** Inertia Wave — portable HIIT / metabolic conditioning tool (battle-rope alternative). Targets functional fitness, CrossFit, HIIT studios, home-gym, sport conditioning.
**Goal:** Identify South African retail buyers/resellers, with named decision makers and contact info where possible.
**Compiled:** May 2026

---

## How to Read This List

Targets are grouped by channel (highest leverage first). Each entry includes the company, why they fit, the most relevant named decision maker we could surface from public sources, and verified general contact details.

> **Note on contacts:** Direct buyer emails are rarely published. Where a named buyer is listed, the recommended approach is a LinkedIn InMail / connection request first, followed by the company's main sales/info inbox citing the buyer's name. Phone numbers and general inboxes are verified from each company's official site as of May 2026.

## Data Limitations

- Apollo.io contact-database access was not enabled on the project's current plan, so direct buyer emails for the large chains (Sportsmans Warehouse, Totalsports, Cape Union Mart, Decathlon, Dis-Chem) had to be sourced from LinkedIn / public profiles. Where individual emails aren't published, the recommended path is a LinkedIn connection + main inbox follow-up.
- Decision-maker titles change frequently — verify each name's current role on LinkedIn before sending.
- Recommended next step if budget allows: an Apollo paid plan, ZoomInfo, or LeadIQ subscription to enrich verified emails for the named buyers above (especially the Tier 1 chain buyers).
